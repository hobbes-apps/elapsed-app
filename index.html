How reminders work

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Android may ask you to allow notifications, sound, and exact alarms. Started does not save a reminder until those settings can produce a visible, audible notification.

Your photos

Photos are temporary markers stored in the app’s private storage. Stop an item to remove its photo and timer. Uninstalling Started removes all remaining app data.
