How reminders work

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. On Android, Started checks notification access, whether its reminder channel is enabled, and exact-alarm access before saving. Alarm volume, Do Not Disturb, and device background restrictions can still affect delivery or sound. Try a short reminder on your device before relying on it.

Your photos

Photos are temporary markers stored in the app’s private storage. Stopping an item hides it from the active list. Started attempts to delete its photo and record after the undo window; failed cleanup is retried when the app opens again. Uninstalling Started removes all remaining app data.
