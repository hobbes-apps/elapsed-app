Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

Started is designed to work privately on your device. Hobbes Apps does not collect, transmit, sell, or share data from the app.

Data stored on your device

When you start an item, Started stores its photo, start time, and optional reminder details in the app’s private storage. This information is not uploaded to Hobbes Apps or a third party. App data is excluded from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Camera

Camera access is used only when you choose to take a temporary time-marker photo. Started does not access your photo library, record audio, or capture photos in the background.

Reminders

Optional reminders use Android or iOS local notification services. Started does not operate a push-notification server and does not receive a device push token. Android may require notification and exact-alarm permission for a precise, audible reminder.

Deletion

Stopping an item removes its photo and timer from active app storage. The app also cleans up orphaned photos. Uninstalling Started removes all remaining app data.

Analytics, accounts, and advertising

Started has no account system, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or in-app purchase system.

Children

Started is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed to children under 13.

Contact
